# Trading Lab Manual Review Packet Template

Date: 2026-06-18

## Purpose

Use this docs-only packet to review one Trading Lab research item end-to-end.
It is paper-only, research-only, and not investment advice.

## Packet Fields

- `packet_id`
- `date`
- `operator`
- `research_item_id`
- `artifact_type`
- `current_status`
- `source_review_summary`
- `watchlist_or_strategy_summary`
- `risk_review_summary`
- `paper_journal_summary`
- `prohibited_language_check`
- `private_data_check`
- `credential_check`
- `execution_path_check`
- `advice_language_check`
- `closeout_status`
- `lessons_learned`
- `next_safe_step`

## Review Checklist

- Public sources are cited or marked HOLD.
- Paper-only intent is explicit.
- No private account data appears.
- No credentials, secrets, keys, tokens, cookies, or `.env` appear.
- No broker/API or order endpoint appears.
- No automated execution appears.
- No production advice appears.
- No data ingestion or generated output appears.
- No app, deployment, or fantasy-lane path appears.
