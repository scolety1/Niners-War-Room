# Formula Gauntlet Revival Lane Setup V0

Verdict: `GREEN_FORMULA_GAUNTLET_REVIVAL_LANE_SETUP_ONLY`

This packet reopens the Formula Gauntlet concept as a future research lane, not as a formula upgrade. It creates a control-plane folder for later work that may generate, test, stress, and review formula candidates after NWR has more source-safe admitted stats, better receipt coverage, and a stronger feature inventory.

## What This Lane Is

- A review-only lane setup packet.
- A guardrail and readiness scaffold for future formula research.
- A place to document future candidate generation, baseline comparison, failure review, ablation, sensitivity, and final review phases before any promotion discussion.
- A reminder that every future candidate must remain review-only unless separately approved by a later production lane.

## What This Lane Is Not

- Not a formula upgrade.
- Not a challenger tournament.
- Not a broad formula search.
- Not a metric admission lane.
- Not a source-promotion lane.
- Not an app, rankings, draft board, trade value, market baseline, outcome-label, or model-score change.
- Not evidence that any missing or unadmitted stat is available for model use.

## Why Reopen It Now

The old historical formula candidate work showed that a sandboxed review-only formula process can be useful when it is carefully bounded. Candidate Search V1 selected `usage_opportunity_volume` for human review only, Candidate Review V1 preserved the evidence without production approval, and Promotion Gate Prep V1 held the candidate because bucket/cutline tradeoffs and elite-player regressions needed human review.

The new revival lane keeps that discipline but does not rerun the old work. It waits for stronger data prerequisites: admitted historical labels, stable identity joins, source-safe metric inventories, exact formula receipt coverage, and leakage-safe baseline definitions.
