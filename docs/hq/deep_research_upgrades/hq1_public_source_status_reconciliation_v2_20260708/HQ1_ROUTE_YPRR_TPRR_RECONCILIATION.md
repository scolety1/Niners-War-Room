# HQ1 Route/YPRR/TPRR Reconciliation

Date: 2026-07-08

## Final Status

True YPRR and TPRR remain blocked. NWR cannot calculate or use those metrics without a safe full routes-run denominator source admitted through a separate future source-admission lane.

No source promotion occurs in this lane.

## Denominator Rules

YPRR requires receiving yards divided by full routes run. TPRR requires targets divided by full routes run. The denominator must be routes run for the player, not a neighboring exposure proxy.

Participation primary-receiver route labels are not enough. They identify route context for a targeted or primary receiver on a play, but they do not count every route run by every eligible receiver.

Snaps are not routes. Offensive snaps can support an exposure proxy only after a safe snap source is admitted and joined safely.

Team pass attempts are not routes. Team pass attempts can support target-share-like proxies, but they cannot become true route denominators.

Public route surfaces are not approved feeds. A visible webpage, leaderboard, or article is not an admitted source unless a future lane proves stable access, usage rights, provenance, identity keys, historical coverage, row grain, and missingness.

## Proxy Rules

Route-like proxies must be labeled proxies. Examples include targets per team pass attempt, receiving yards per team pass attempt, first downs per target, first downs per team pass attempt, air yards per team pass attempt, targets per offensive snap, and receiving first downs per offensive snap.

Proxy metrics must not be described as YPRR, TPRR, route share, route participation, or routes-run metrics.

## Future Admission Requirements

A route-source admission lane must produce a source contract, field dictionary, source provenance/checksum trail, licensing review, stable historical coverage matrix, player identity join audit, row-grain proof, missingness report, leakage/as-of audit, blocked-field scan, and review-only backtest readiness gate.

Until that happens, true route denominator metrics remain `RED_ROUTE_DENOMINATOR_UNSAFE`.
