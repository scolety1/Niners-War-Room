# Trade Summary Contract

Trading Lab authority remains `MANUAL_DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY`.

`Trade at a glance` shows exact side membership, production rank, position rank, value band when present, age, rookie/research status, individual market context with freshness, a small applicable Outcome subset, personal context, and the major owner-facing caveat.

`How the sides differ` may compare directly derivable production standing, evidence counts, rookie uncertainty, career window, positions, pick flexibility, and the highest individual display-authorized market value. It never sums a side or assigns weights.

`Bottom line` classifies the exchange and names the strongest production-ranked asset on each side, prospect-only authority, and supported lifecycle caveats. It cannot state winner, fair/unfair, accept/reject, package value, or grade.

The current selectors, displayed summary, saved scenario, reopened scenario, Markdown, and JSON must remain asset-for-asset and side-for-side identical.
