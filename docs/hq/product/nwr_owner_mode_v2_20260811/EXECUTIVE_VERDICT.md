# NWR Owner Mode V2 executive verdict

## Would the owner actually enjoy using NWR now?

**MOSTLY.**

The default Dynasty product now starts with football decisions instead of model operations. Home,
Rankings, Market Analysis, Why NWR Ranks Them, Player Compare, Player Detail, Rookie Review,
Dynasty Outcomes, Analyze Trade, Scenario Playground, Decision Tracker, My Board, and All Dynasty
Assets form one owner-facing workflow. Dense production and comparison tables remain available as
Rankings Data and Compare Data under Advanced / Data.

No governed rank, score, probability, market value, rookie formula, research output, or Redraft
projection changed. Owner Mode translates existing receipts; unavailable evidence stays unavailable.

The verdict is not YES because the preserved Trade Analyzer still becomes dense below its primary
decision path, the display-only market baseline is stale as of 2026-07-17, and a bookmarked direct
route can show Streamlit's dismissible "Page not found" notice during the first cold session after a
server restart even though the registered page then renders correctly.
