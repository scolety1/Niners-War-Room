# Asset Explorer repair

Asset Explorer is the full governed-asset catalog, not another ranking page. It covers 240 current players, 73 scored rookies, 7 blocked rookies, 50 2026 draft picks, and 9 future-pick contexts while preserving separate authority and scales.

The crash was caused by five count categories and four fixed columns under `zip(..., strict=True)`. Counts now render in bounded batches sized to the remaining categories. Search, filters, detail links, and read-only behavior remain intact.
