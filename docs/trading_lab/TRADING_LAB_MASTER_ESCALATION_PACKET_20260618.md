# Trading Lab Master Escalation Packet

Date: 2026-06-18

## Purpose

This packet escalates Trading Lab to Master HQ as a safe research foundation
that should remain frozen until Master or the user explicitly opens a new phase.

## Safe Capabilities

- Manual source inventory review.
- Manual source policy/license checklist.
- Research intake.
- Watchlist notes.
- Strategy notes.
- Risk journals.
- Paper journals.
- Manual lifecycle validation.
- Manual review packets.
- No-advice rewrite guidance.
- Blocked-work gate screening.

## Blocked Capabilities

- Data ingestion or market-data fetching.
- Backtesting or simulation implementation.
- Broker/API integration.
- Credentials, secrets, keys, or tokens.
- Real-money trading or broker orders.
- Automated execution.
- Production investment advice.
- App wiring or deployment.
- Generated market datasets or outputs.
- Private brokerage/account data.
- Fantasy-lane changes.

## Required Future Approval

Any future phase must name:

- Phase purpose.
- Allowed paths.
- Prohibited paths.
- Validation commands.
- Whether generated outputs are permitted.
- Whether source/data policy has been reviewed.
- Closeout requirements.

Without explicit approval, blocked work remains blocked.

## Recommended Master Decision

Mark Trading Lab GREEN for manual paper/research use and HOLD for any future
implementation phase.

## Safe Maintenance Options

- Docs-only glossary refresh.
- Docs-only operator walkthrough using fake examples.
- Validation-only status constant review.
- Manual source policy checklist refinement.

## Warning

Do not open data/backtest/broker/deploy work from this packet. It is a handoff
and freeze artifact, not an implementation approval.
