# Supported Capabilities and Known Limitations

Supported: Finished V1 rankings, governed Outcome context, Review-Only rookie
board, typed asset search, player comparison, manual descriptive trade review,
draft workflows, local planning, Data Health, receipt-backed persistence, and
backup/recovery controls.

Limitations: no accepted rookie-veteran common scale; no automatic trade
recommendation; no automated roster hydration; seven rookies remain blocked on
exact identity; personal board overlay and Decision Journal remain deferred;
optional paid/provider and market-retention lanes are closed as not required.
