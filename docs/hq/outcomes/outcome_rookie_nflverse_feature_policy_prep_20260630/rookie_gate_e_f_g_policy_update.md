# Rookie Gate E/F/G Policy Update After NFLVerse Context Rebuild

## Executive Position

Gate G remains blocked.

The rebuilt NFLVerse player context artifact and approved identity binding apply
packet improve review/display coverage, but they do not approve rookie model
training, active rookie probabilities, app-facing rookie columns, or Rankings
wiring.

## Gate E

Gate E remains a model R&D / feasibility concept only. This policy prep packet
does not approve:

- model training;
- model tuning;
- feature promotion;
- source-truth promotion;
- active probability generation.

NFLVerse features that look promising for review, such as draft capital,
combine, depth chart role, last active season/week, snap recency, and roster
status, must pass future policy gates before any Gate E model work.

## Gate F

Gate F may continue to support review-only drafted-player coverage reporting
where positive draft-pick evidence exists. It does not become model-ready from
this packet.

The 41 newly bound rows improve identity review coverage, but missing non-
identity context remains `Not enough information`.

## Gate G

Gate G remains closed.

Still blocked:

- active rookie probabilities;
- app-facing rookie Outcome columns;
- Rankings integration;
- Player Compare / Live Draft / Mock Draft behavior changes;
- source-truth promotion;
- model/training use;
- hidden sorting or rank changes.

Gate G can reopen only through a separate user-approved release lane after
coverage, validation, feature policy, source policy, missingness, leakage, and
UI risk are all green.

## UDFA And CFBD

UDFA modeling remains blocked. Draft absence, roster appearance, player_stats
appearance, snap appearance, depth chart appearance, or injury context cannot
confirm UDFA status.

CFBD remains review-only unless future human-approved identity and source gates
explicitly approve stronger use. This packet does not approve CFBD model or
training input.
