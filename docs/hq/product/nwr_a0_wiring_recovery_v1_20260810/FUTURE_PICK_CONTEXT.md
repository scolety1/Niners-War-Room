# Future Pick Context

The bounded contract adds 2027, 2028, and 2029 first-, second-, and third-round assets. Each asset carries year, round, unknown slot, high uncertainty, a round-only draft-context tier, context-only authority, and an explicit ban on player-value equivalence.

The CSV source is `config/nwr_future_pick_context_v1.csv`. It contains no numeric player-equivalent value, early/mid/late guess, owner guess, recommendation, or hidden sort field. These assets are searchable, addable to Trading Lab, persistable, reopenable, and exportable.
