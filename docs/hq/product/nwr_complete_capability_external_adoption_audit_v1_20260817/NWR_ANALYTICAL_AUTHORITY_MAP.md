# NWR Analytical Authority Map

| Domain | Authority | Permitted role | Prohibited role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dynasty veteran rank | Finished V1 | Production ordering and veteran decision anchor | Must not be overwritten by Market, Research or Redraft |
| Rookie evaluation | Rookie Review | Canonical scored rookie pool; review-only visibility separately labeled | Unscored visibility rows cannot become ranked truth |
| Outcomes | Outcome V3 | Outcome distributions/scenarios | Not a replacement rank |
| Research | Unified Research | Medium-term context, uncertainty and cited evidence | Never silently promoted into a production score |
| Dynasty market | DynastyProcess snapshot | Display-only 1QB market corroboration/gap | Not NWR rank; not Redraft ADP; never ingest opaque repository contents |
| Redraft | Redraft Champion projection/replacement engine | League-scored projection, replacement value, rank and confidence | Must not rewrite Dynasty production ranks |
| Rookie-veteran compare | Multi-authority bridge | Show immediate Redraft, Dynasty rank, rookie review and research side by side | No fake universal cross-authority score |
| Trades | Trade Decision Assistant | Evidence dimensions, roster/window fit, accept/counter/reject family | No universal package sum pretending certainty |
| K/DST | External consensus boundary | Optional week-specific consensus with source/freshness | No hidden NWR schedule/weather/injury model |
| Owner data | Local owner workspace | My Board, decisions, scenarios, draft state, profiles | External providers cannot mutate it |

## Required display order

Every combined player surface must preserve labels for `Official`, `Market`, `War Room`, and `My Rank` (or the mode-specific equivalent). Missing fields remain `UNKNOWN` or `NOT_ENOUGH_INFORMATION`. Research is contextual. Market is corroborative. Neither is a back door into canonical ranking.

## Cross-mode rule

Dynasty and Redraft may share identity, provider receipts, league state and owner storage contracts. They must not share a single composite rank. Cross-mode compare is a bridge of explicitly named authorities.
