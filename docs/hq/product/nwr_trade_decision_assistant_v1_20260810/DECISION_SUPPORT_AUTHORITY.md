# Decision Support Authority

Authority: `ADVISORY_DECISION_SUPPORT`.

NWR may prefer a side, recommend accept/reject/counter, state confidence, explain named
evidence, describe what would change the result, and generate verified roster-aware counter
structures. The owner remains the decision authority.

NWR may not claim certainty, change source ranks, blend authorities into a secret score,
invent ownership, or execute a trade. This supersedes `MANUAL_DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY` only for the
new Trade Decision surface. Existing manual planners retain their narrower authority.
