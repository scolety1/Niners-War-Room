# Final Owner Navigation

## Dynasty app

- **Home** — roster state, decisions due, freshness and recent work.
- **Rankings & Market** — NWR rank, Market context, My Board; authorities visibly separate.
- **Players** — Detail, Compare, Rookie Review, Outcomes, research context.
- **Trades** — Analyzer, Finder, Opponent Targets, Counteroffers, history.
- **Team** — Roster, needs and later approved weekly/waiver workflows.
- **Plan** — Roster Planner, future picks, upcoming draft/keeper/drop/trade-deadline prep, scenarios, decisions.
- **System** — sources, freshness, backups/import/export and Development/Labs.

## Redraft app

- **Home** — league/profile, draft or weekly phase, freshness and next action.
- **Draft** — Prep, Rankings, Beat ADP, Mock, Live Board, draft history.
- **Weekly** — Lineup, Waivers, Streamers, bye/injury replacement.
- **Players** — Detail, Compare, NWR versus ADP/market evidence.
- **League & Data** — workspace, scoring, roster, sources and imports.
- **System** — backups, receipts and Development/Labs.

Trade tools enter Redraft navigation only after owner value is proven. Technical/lab names never appear as primary Owner tasks.
