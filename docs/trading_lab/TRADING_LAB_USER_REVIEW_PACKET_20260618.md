# Trading Lab User Review Packet - 2026-06-18

## How To Open

Open the isolated Streamlit page: `app/pages/11_trade_lab.py`.

## What To Click First

1. Start with `Trade For Player`.
2. Review the best trade card.
3. Review ranked packages.
4. Open the negotiation ladder.
5. Switch to `Trade Away Player`.
6. Review the target board categories.
7. Check Training Mode.
