## How To Use The Ranks

- Candidate Rank: best review-only rookie/veteran comparison available tonight. Use it to find who deserves discussion.
- Final Board Rank: frozen approved baseline. It remains visible and unchanged, especially for the original 66-player pool.
- Prefer Candidate Rank over Final Board Rank when a known veteran like Zay/Olave/Jamo is clearly lifted by internal dynasty/tuned evidence and the human caveat is acceptable.
- Prefer Final Board Rank when Candidate confidence is Low, source notes say Not enough information, or the player is PDF-only without internal value.
- ADP/range/current pick value: display-only price context. It can help avoid paying too early, but it is not model truth and not an internal value input.
- Outcome/horizon: display-only and partial. Missing Outcome means Not enough information, not zero.
