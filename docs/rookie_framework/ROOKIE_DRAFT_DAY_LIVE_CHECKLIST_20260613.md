## Emergency Stop Rules

Stop the rookie decision and choose `hold`, `trade_down`, `needs_data`, or `pass` if any of these are true:

- No clear uncapped player is available.
- A premium candidate has unresolved injury risk.
- A premium candidate has a source conflict that changes role, injury, usage, or target-earning interpretation.
- The profile depends only on soft secondary charting.
- The player has no clear role path.
- The player is generic, safe, and low-upside.
- The player needs ADP, ranking, projection, consensus, market, trade value, or draft-kit language to make the case.
- The live choice would require production rankings, probabilities, bands, app wiring, or veteran outcome heads.
