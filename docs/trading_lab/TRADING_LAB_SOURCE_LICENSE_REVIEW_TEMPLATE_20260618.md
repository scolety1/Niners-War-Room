# Trading Lab Source License Review Template

Date: 2026-06-18

## Disclaimer

This template is not legal advice. It is a manual research gate for deciding
whether a source can be referenced in paper/research-only Trading Lab work.

## Fields

- source_name:
- public_private_status:
- attribution_requirements:
- license_terms_review_status:
- redistribution_storage_notes:
- refresh_cadence:
- sensitivity:
- approved_use:
- prohibited_use:
- review_status: ACCEPT / HOLD / REJECT
- reviewer_notes:

## HOLD Criteria

- Terms unclear.
- License unclear.
- Redistribution/storage rules unclear.
- Paid or private source requires later explicit approval.
- Review required before use.

## REJECT Criteria

- Broker credentials.
- Account keys, secrets, or tokens.
- Private brokerage/account exports.
- Real-money execution data.
- Private account balances.

## ACCEPT Criteria

- Public manual reference.
- Attribution is recorded.
- Storage policy is compatible with docs-only/manual use.
- No private account data, secrets, or execution path.
