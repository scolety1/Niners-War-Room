## Verdict

The product reads as a professional sports analytics command center: deep near-black foundation, violet Dynasty identity, restrained gold Redraft identity, crisp crimson/cyan state accents, consistent custom chrome, dense but navigable panels, and a strong owner-first hierarchy. Dynasty and Redraft are unmistakably separate.

## Improvements verified

- Owner-readable density floor and controls hold at 1440 × 920.
- Long boards use bounded depth controls, sticky table context, sorting, filters, and reset.
- Draft Room emphasizes the live board and gives explicit mutation confirmation.
- Player Detail separates admitted rank, research range signals, display-only market, risk, and reasons.
- Normal Data Health no longer leads with hashes, loopback addresses, or machine enum labels.
- Blocked assets are visibly blocked and decision actions are unavailable.
- Global palette supports keyboard selection, focus restoration, focus containment, and full governed search.

## Residual visual/UX opportunities

- The app intentionally remains dense; users who prefer a low-density consumer UI may want a future compact/comfortable toggle.
- The frozen-sidecar startup interval is visible as a short branded loading state.
- Some decision evidence remains lengthy by design and benefits from progressive disclosure in a future polish pass.
- Historical team abbreviations are shown as admitted by their source packets; a future identity-layer normalization could make display codes more uniform without rewriting source evidence.
