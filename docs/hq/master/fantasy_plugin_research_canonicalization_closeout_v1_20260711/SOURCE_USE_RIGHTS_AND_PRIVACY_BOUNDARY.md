# Source Use Rights and Privacy Boundary

## Policy established before canonical reconstruction

This policy controls every repository-facing artifact in this closeout. The source commit and its private archive are read-only restricted evidence. Nothing in this packet admits either plugin as a production source or changes NWR source governance.

## Permitted canonical content

- Aggregate test counts and classifications.
- Sanitized capability and reliability conclusions.
- Manual-use guardrails, integration decisions, and reentry triggers.
- Source inventory expressed only as category counts.
- Public provider and product names needed to identify the evaluated capability.

## Excluded content

Repository-facing artifacts must not contain exact connected league names or identifiers, recurring league identifiers, owner names or identifiers, provider team names, reversible owner/team keys, complete private roster membership, private transactions, private draft identifiers, account email addresses, credentials, authorization headers, cookies, OAuth or session tokens, raw receipt JSON, local private receipt paths, substantial provider output, or provider-generated explanations copied from results.

Player names are omitted because aggregate reporting is sufficient. Raw receipt hashes are also omitted because canonical governance does not require them.

## Rights boundary

The audit established no written permission for persistent provider-output storage, display, retention, redistribution, or downstream production use. Unknown downstream rights remain a blocker and are not permission. Manual consultation is permitted only subject to applicable provider terms, privacy obligations, the operating guide in this packet, and no persistent reproduction of substantial output.

## Handling rule

The original local source commit and private ZIP remain the restricted evidence archive. They must remain local-only and must not be merged, cherry-picked, fast-forwarded, pushed, or copied wholesale. This packet is a selective reconstruction from conclusions, not a derived raw-data export.
