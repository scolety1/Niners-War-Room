# Trading Lab Language Guardrail Audit

Required page and memo disclaimer:

Manual planning only. No trade valuation, market valuation, pick valuation, model score, or automatic recommendation is calculated.

## Removed From Active User Flow

- directional package verdicts
- visible-score gap language
- market sanity panel language
- package-level market totals
- pick pricing language
- offer-generation framing

## Allowed Negative Assertions

The page and memo may mention blocked behavior only to say it is not calculated or not present.

Allowed examples:

- No trade valuation
- No market valuation
- No pick valuation
- No model score
- No automatic recommendation
- Not an offer generator

## Blocked Active Claims

The active page, service output, and memo output must not claim:

- a trade is favorable, fair, risky, won, or lost
- a minimum offer exists
- a pick has a calculated value
- a market source decides package quality
- a checklist status changes package quality
- missing evidence is zero, clean, healthy, positive, or neutral
