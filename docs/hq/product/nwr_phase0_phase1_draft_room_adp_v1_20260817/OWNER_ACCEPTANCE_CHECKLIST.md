# Owner Acceptance Checklist

1. Open the installed **Niners War Room — Redraft** app.
2. Confirm `Fantasy Gamers` is active and shows `2026 · 10-Team PPR · 1QB`.
3. Open Draft Room, choose slot 9 and Normal, then select Start Draft.
4. Confirm picks 1–8 are CPU selections and pick 9 is highlighted for you.
5. Draft from a recommendation or Available Players and confirm CPU teams stop at your next pick.
6. Click team headers, including your highlighted column, and inspect rosters.
7. Confirm drafted players remain on the board/log but disappear from availability.
8. Try Undo, one CPU pick, advance to my pick, and restart.
9. Confirm K/DST are real selectable assets labeled manual/not modeled.
10. Optionally import an owner ADP CSV and verify NWR rank does not move while Beat ADP/timing activates.

The app was not launched or controlled during installation.
