# Evidence Hierarchy

1. Finished V1 production ranks, position ranks, bands, age, confidence, and lifecycle flags.
2. Rookie Review and Unified Research evidence, kept separate from Finished V1.
3. Governed future-pick year/round/slot context, with no player-equivalent value.
4. Applicable Outcome V3 context.
5. Market DP evidence as stale secondary corroboration or negotiation context only.
6. League format and explicit team-window context as interpretation, never rank mutation.

Missing evidence remains unknown. It is never converted to zero.

Roster ownership is sourced read-only from the validated active pack's `fact_rosters.csv` and
`fact_future_picks.csv`. Specific counters require exact same-snapshot ownership for every
asset on both sides.
