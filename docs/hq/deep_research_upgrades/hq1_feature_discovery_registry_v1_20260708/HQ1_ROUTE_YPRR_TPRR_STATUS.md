# HQ1 Route/YPRR/TPRR Status

Date: 2026-07-08

## Clear Status

True YPRR and TPRR remain blocked for NWR until a safe full routes-run source exists and is admitted through a separate source-admission lane.

This packet does not admit any route source. It does not calculate route metrics. It does not promote public route pages, commercial feeds, scraped proprietary surfaces, or partial route labels.

## Why True Route Metrics Are Blocked

YPRR requires receiving yards divided by routes run. TPRR requires targets divided by routes run. The denominator is full routes run, not team pass attempts, offensive snaps, pass plays, targets, receptions, or primary-receiver route labels.

The HQ1 research corpus did not identify a broad, public, reproducible, historically stable, license-safe, join-safe feed for full routes run across relevant NFL seasons and player positions.

## Participation Data Boundary

nflverse participation data is valuable for review context, but its primary-receiver route labels are not full routes-run denominators. Those labels can help identify route concepts for the player targeted on a play, but they do not establish how many routes every eligible player ran.

Therefore participation data may support route-like research questions, but it cannot unlock true YPRR, true TPRR, or true route share by itself.

## Public Route Surface Boundary

Public route/YPRR/TPRR surfaces may appear on the open web. They remain unapproved unless a future lane proves all of the following:

- Reproducible access without scraping prohibited or unstable proprietary surfaces.
- Clear license or usage permission for NWR review artifacts.
- Stable historical coverage by season, player, position, and team.
- Durable row grain and field definitions.
- Join-safe identity keys beyond name-only matching.
- Clear source timestamps and as-of rules.
- Missingness and coverage bias reporting.
- No proprietary grades, projections, ranks, or analyst-derived fields mixed into factual route fields.

## Allowed Route-Like Proxies

Route-like proxies may be researched when they do not require full routes run and when their input sources pass the relevant future gate. Examples preserved in the backlog include:

- Targets per team pass attempt.
- Receiving yards per team pass attempt.
- Receiving first downs per team pass attempt.
- First downs per target.
- Air yards per team pass attempt.
- Target share.
- Air-yards share.
- Targets per offensive snap, only if snap source and identity joins are safe.
- Receiving first downs per offensive snap, only if snap source and identity joins are safe.

These proxies must be labeled as proxies. They must not be described as YPRR, TPRR, route share, route participation, or routes-run metrics.

## Future Source-Admission Requirements

A future route-source admission lane must produce a source contract, source status matrix, field dictionary, identity join audit, coverage matrix, missingness report, leakage/as-of audit, licensing review, and blocked-field scan before any route denominator can be used in review-only scoring.

Production promotion would still require a later separate model/source promotion lane after review-only validation. Admission to review does not equal model approval.
