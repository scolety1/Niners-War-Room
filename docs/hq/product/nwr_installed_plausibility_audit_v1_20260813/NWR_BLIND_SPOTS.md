# NWR blind spots

| Blind spot | What NWR knows | What it does not reliably know | Likely consequence | Owner verification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Late injury/status news | Prior production, age, governed status snapshot | News after the snapshot | Role/value can remain too high or too low | Current team injury report and credible beat reporting |
| Sudden depth-chart or team change | Historical role and production | New competition, trade, release, scheme change | Opportunity persistence can be stale | Current depth chart, snaps, routes, coach usage |
| Missing route metrics | Production and any governed research fields | Target earning/separation for unavailable players | Volume/efficiency inference becomes less discriminating | Routes, YPRR, target rate, alignment |
| Unresolved rookie identity | Raw row and blocker reason | Safe cross-source identity join | Prospect remains blocked or confidence-capped | NFL draft record, team roster, canonical ID mapping |
| Stale market snapshot | July 17 ranks and sign-correct gaps | Current transaction price | Large gaps can be obsolete | Current 1QB market and recent completed league deals |
| Future-pick ownership/slot | Pick year and round | Owner, contender status, exact probability | No safe named counter; pick packages look coarse | League pick ledger and plausible slot distribution |
| Format mismatch | Installed 10-team 1QB rules | External source's hidden assumptions | QB/TE gaps appear larger than they are | Verify 1QB/SF, TEP, PPR, lineup depth |
| New role with low sample | Prior production and metadata | Stable future workload | Veteran or breakout extrapolation can overshoot | Camp/season usage and injury-adjusted opportunity |
| Optional packaged market context | Installed display artifact | Guaranteed equality with isolated facade fallback | Reproductions can show different stale market ranks | Treat installed UI as authority; record artifact provenance |
| Coarse calibration buckets | Governed outcome family/tier | Fully individualized event probabilities | Multiple players show identical percentages | Use as bands, not exact bespoke odds |

The most important blind spot is **fresh role context**. A large NWR rank gap with missing current team, injury, route, or workload data deserves manual verification before any trade.
