# Product Integration Result

Authority: `RESEARCH_ONLY_NOT_PRODUCTION`

The frozen unified research ordering is exposed additively as **Unified Dynasty
Preview — Research Only**. Finished V1 remains the first selector option and the
default production authority. Rookie Review remains a separate review-only
authority.

- Rankings: explicit three-authority selector, frozen preview board, blocked rows,
  and rookie/veteran neighborhoods.
- Player Compare: source-separated evidence is preserved; Unified Research Context
  and rookie neighborhoods are additional context only.
- Asset Explorer: Unified Research Rank is hidden by default and can be enabled and
  used as an explicit research-only sort.
- Trading Lab: optional collapsed research context only; trade construction and
  descriptive logic do not consume the research rank.

No model was fitted, tuned, or manually reordered for this integration.
