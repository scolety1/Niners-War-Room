# Definition of Finish

NWR is complete when all ten Golden Lane phase gates are resolved and Phase 8 authorizes GREEN_NWR_GOLDEN_RELEASE_COMPLETE.

## Model authority

- Finished V1, rookie, and rookie-veteran comparison authority are explicit.
- Win Now and two-/three-year football value are admitted or formally rejected.
- Availability is calibrated or remains visibly factual context.
- Market retention is separate and admitted or formally closed.

## Product authority

- Rankings, Unified Board, Player Compare, Trading Lab, draft tools, roster planning, and Data Health use governed, identity-stable sources.
- Review-only and production values remain distinct; blocked identities remain blocked.
- No opaque recommendation or hidden source blend exists; invalid inputs fail closed.

## Operational authority

- Deterministic regeneration, security, Hermetic, LocalData, Data Health, desktop lifecycle, rollback, recovery, and documentation acceptance pass.
- Scheduled refresh is approved, disabled, or absent/fail-closed.
- Stable is synchronized and no candidate regression is hidden.

A null model result is compatible with completion; no new formula is required merely to declare progress.
