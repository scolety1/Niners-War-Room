# Stop and No-Recreate List

## Immediate stops

Stop the proposed Trading Lab lane if it requires or produces any of the following:

- a ranking, formula, projection, value, score, package total, fairness verdict, winner, recommendation, or automated offer;
- a source registry change, source promotion, new endpoint, or provider call;
- Flaim or FantasyBot content, integration, adapter, or numerical influence;
- rookie registry reads/writes, queue closure, mapping, identity resolution, player/evidence rows, locator, or authority changes;
- Live Draft or Mock Draft pick/event/state changes;
- frozen PYF, GAUNTLET_081, current-board, or prospective artifact access or mutation;
- silent autosave, implicit overwrite, arbitrary user-selected storage paths, or persistence of derived source facts;
- application, service, test, or data changes outside the future lane's explicit contract.

## No-recreate interpretation

A later lane may fix a reproduced defect in a completed surface, but it must cite the defect and preserve the completed contract. A broad wish to improve, modernize, consolidate, or clean up is not evidence that completed functionality should be rebuilt.

The old post-formula ordering and superseded rookie batch prompts are historical records, not active work instructions.
