# Future Formula Gauntlet Flow

Status for every phase in this document: `NOT_RUN_IN_THIS_LANE`

This document describes the future intended process only. It does not execute, approve, tune, promote, or activate anything.

## Phase 1: Candidate Generation

Status: `NOT_RUN_IN_THIS_LANE`

Future work may generate bounded, interpretable candidate formulas using only admitted or review-approved fields. Candidate families must be declared before evaluation.

Required future artifacts:

- Candidate formula registry.
- Candidate input and source-status matrix.
- Receipt dependency map.
- Blocked-field exclusion report.

## Phase 2: Baseline Comparison

Status: `NOT_RUN_IN_THIS_LANE`

Future candidates must be compared to a baseline ladder, including prior-year finish, current-formula-family proxy, and exact current formula replay if replay becomes possible.

Required future artifacts:

- Baseline definition table.
- Baseline metric report.
- Candidate versus baseline metric report.

## Phase 3: Prior-Year Finish Comparison

Status: `NOT_RUN_IN_THIS_LANE`

Prior-year finish remains a serious champion baseline and must not be hand-waved away.

Required future artifacts:

- Prior-year finish baseline receipt.
- Position-level deltas.
- Top-N and startable precision deltas.

## Phase 4: Archetype Stress Testing

Status: `NOT_RUN_IN_THIS_LANE`

Future candidates must be tested against player archetypes such as volume backs, low-prior-opportunity breakouts, older WRs, elite QBs, fragile roles, and TE route/target dependency.

Required future artifacts:

- Archetype definitions.
- Archetype impact report.
- Regression casebook.

## Phase 5: Position-Specific Tests

Status: `NOT_RUN_IN_THIS_LANE`

Future candidates must be evaluated separately for QB, RB, WR, and TE, with K excluded unless a separate reason exists.

Required future artifacts:

- Position-level metrics.
- Position-level cutline harm report.
- Position-specific caveat map.

## Phase 6: Cutline Harm Testing

Status: `NOT_RUN_IN_THIS_LANE`

Future candidates must identify whether useful starters or actual cutline hits are moved below practical decision cutlines.

Required future artifacts:

- Startable bucket tradeoff report.
- Largest harm casebook.
- Human review checklist.

## Phase 7: Ablation

Status: `NOT_RUN_IN_THIS_LANE`

Future candidates must be decomposed to show which feature groups actually help.

Required future artifacts:

- Ablation matrix.
- Feature-group contribution report.
- Remove-one and add-one sensitivity where appropriate.

## Phase 8: Sensitivity

Status: `NOT_RUN_IN_THIS_LANE`

Future candidates must prove they are not brittle to missingness, position mix, season window, and source-policy assumptions.

Required future artifacts:

- Missingness sensitivity report.
- Season split sensitivity report.
- Source inclusion/exclusion sensitivity report.

## Phase 9: Failure Review

Status: `NOT_RUN_IN_THIS_LANE`

Future candidates must undergo a failure autopsy before any finalist decision.

Required future artifacts:

- False-positive taxonomy.
- False-negative taxonomy.
- Player casebook.
- Human review questions.

## Phase 10: Formula Anatomy

Status: `NOT_RUN_IN_THIS_LANE`

Future finalist candidates must be explainable in plain English and by component.

Required future artifacts:

- Formula anatomy report.
- Input and weight explanation.
- Source receipt map.

## Phase 11: Champion Surgery Or Hybridization

Status: `NOT_RUN_IN_THIS_LANE`

Future work may propose safe hybrid candidates only after failure review. Hybridization is not promotion.

Required future artifacts:

- Surgery proposal.
- Guardrail impact report.
- Rejected-change log.

## Phase 12: Final Gauntlet

Status: `NOT_RUN_IN_THIS_LANE`

Future finalists must rerun the full comparison, stress, ablation, sensitivity, and failure-review package.

Required future artifacts:

- Final leaderboard.
- Finalist evidence packet.
- Non-promotion notice.

## Phase 13: Human Approval Gate

Status: `NOT_RUN_IN_THIS_LANE`

The future gauntlet stops before promotion. Any production discussion requires a separate human-approved lane.

Required future artifacts:

- Human approval checklist.
- Source promotion status audit.
- Production-path impact audit.
- Explicit stop-before-promotion verdict.
