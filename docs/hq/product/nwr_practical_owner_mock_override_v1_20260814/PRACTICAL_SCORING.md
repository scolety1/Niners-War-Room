# APPROXIMATE_SCORING

Owner-facing copy: **NWR models the major QB/RB/WR/TE scoring rules for Fantasy Gamers. Five uncommon scoring events are not included. Kicker and DST are manual/unmodeled.**

The omitted events are `pass_2pt`, `rush_2pt`, `rec_2pt`, `fum_rec`, and `fum_rec_td`. Their absence is a model limitation, not a claim that they never occur or are worth zero in Sleeper.

The board name is **NWR Fantasy Gamers Practical Rankings**, not an exact-profile ranking.
