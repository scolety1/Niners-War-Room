# CPU ADP Behavior

When a usable FFC snapshot exists, CPU teams draft from ADP-centered ordering with seeded variation, roster legality, positional needs, roster construction, and deterministic late K/DST completion. They log `CPU_MARKET_ADP_FFC` and do not become NWR clone drafters.

The verified slot-9 smoke mock completed 150 unique legal selections. Of those, 117 CPU selections logged `CPU_MARKET_ADP_FFC`, 13 owner turns used the NWR recommendation path, and 20 K/DST completions logged `MANUAL_UNMODELED`. The disclosed NWR-order fallback remains available only when ADP is unavailable.
