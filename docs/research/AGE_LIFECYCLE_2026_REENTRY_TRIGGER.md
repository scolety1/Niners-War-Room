# Age lifecycle 2026 re-entry trigger

Current state: WAITING_FOR_ADMITTED_2026_OUTCOMES

The age lane may re-enter review only after real 2026 outcomes have been
formally admitted under the repository's evidence, identity, leakage, and
rights controls. A calendar date alone is not a trigger.

Required trigger evidence:

1. admitted 2026 outcomes with exact source and receipt authority;
2. exact player and season identities with unresolved joins rejected;
3. immutable pre-outcome predictions or a documented inability to replay;
4. leakage-safe evaluation that excludes future and current-board values;
5. position/cohort coverage sufficient for repeated-position analysis;
6. preregistered acceptance gates and a frozen no-age baseline;
7. review-only candidate outputs with no production write path.

After the trigger, rerun baseline reproduction, walk-forward or leave-one-season
out testing, coverage, false-positive, materiality, and position-harm checks.
Independent review is required before any candidate can be discussed for
production.

Until every item is satisfied:

- do not synthesize outcomes;
- do not admit an age candidate;
- do not change the 240-row board;
- do not change rankings, formulas, values, or age curves;
- do not call providers merely to unblock the lane.
