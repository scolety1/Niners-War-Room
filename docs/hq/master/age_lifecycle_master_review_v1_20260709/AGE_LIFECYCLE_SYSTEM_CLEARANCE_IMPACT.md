# Age / Lifecycle System Clearance Impact

## Clearance Change

Age/lifecycle is admitted for `REVIEW_ONLY_FORMULA_PILOT_CONTEXT`.

## What This Unlocks

- A future small review-only formula pilot contract may include age/lifecycle context.
- Age/lifecycle may be used for miss taxonomy, prior-decline review, breakout-window review, sparse-history guardrails, and position-specific slice reporting.

## What This Does Not Unlock

- Exact Model v4 historical replay
- Formula Gauntlet tournaments
- 100-candidate Gauntlet
- Champion refinement
- Rankings integration
- Production/model-use
- Source promotion
- Production accuracy claims

## Current Gates

- Exact Model v4 replay remains blocked.
- Formula Gauntlet tournaments remain blocked.
- 100-candidate Formula Gauntlet remains blocked.
- Champion refinement remains blocked.
- Rankings integration remains blocked.
- Production/model-use remains blocked.

## Reason

The signal is meaningful as context but not as a standalone predictive score. Age/lifecycle must remain paired with PYF, role archetype, sparse-history, and position-level reporting.
