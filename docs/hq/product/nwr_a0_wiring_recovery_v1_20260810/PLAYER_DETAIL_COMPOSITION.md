# Player Detail Composition

Player Detail is composition, not a new model. One exact governed asset selector combines:

- identity and authority from the governed registry;
- Finished V1 rank/score/card receipts for current players;
- Rookie Review rank/evidence for rookies;
- Unified Research context with an explicit Research Only banner;
- position-applicable Outcome V3 rows;
- lawful market context when joined;
- populated score/gate/evidence receipts;
- read-only Personal Board context;
- caveats and advanced hashes/provenance.

Blocked prospects and picks render their governed status even without scores. Page open performs no writes.
