# Team-Window Behavior

The page states `Analyzing for: CONTENDING`, `BALANCED`, or `REBUILDING`. Existing Personal
Board team-window entries seed the default; reopened saved trades restore their saved window;
the selector is a trade-specific override.

- Contending prioritizes established production, moderated by lifecycle cautions.
- Balanced combines best-asset band, youth/runway, and pick class.
- Rebuilding prioritizes youth, lifecycle runway, and earlier future flexibility.

Only D8 changes. Source ranks, scores, rookie evidence, pick evidence, and market evidence are
unchanged. The owner case remains `COUNTER — MEDIUM` in all three views; D8 favors the incoming
side for Contending and the current side for Balanced/Rebuilding.
