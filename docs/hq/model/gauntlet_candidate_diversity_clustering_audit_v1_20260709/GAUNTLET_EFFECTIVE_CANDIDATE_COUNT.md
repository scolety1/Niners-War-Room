# Gauntlet Effective Candidate Count

The raw Gauntlet registered `120` candidates and scored `114`. Of those, `90` score-changing candidates beat PYF overall.

The clustering audit reduces that to:

- Output-correlation clusters: `7`
- Promising clusters: `6`
- Genuinely distinct promising clusters: `6`
- Effective independent candidate count: `6`

Answer to the key question: it was not 90 independent formulas beating PYF. It was `6` useful independent neighborhoods, with many near-duplicates or same-neighborhood variants around multi-year production.
