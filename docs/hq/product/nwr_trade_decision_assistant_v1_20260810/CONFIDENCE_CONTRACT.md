# Confidence Contract

Confidence is `HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, or `LOW` and is distinct from recommendation strength.

It depends on evidence coverage, Finished V1 authority coverage, agreement among dimensions,
rookie/pick uncertainty, explicit conflicts, and stale market context. Missing or blocked
evidence lowers confidence; it never adds a neutral or zero input.

The owner case is `MEDIUM`: Finished V1 anchors and governed pick classes are available, but
Chris Bell is outside the production scale, both pick slots are unknown, market evidence is
stale, and roster ownership is incomplete.
