## Interpretation

The expanded historical window materially improves sample size. It also changes
the RB 5Y calibration picture:

- `RB_T12_WITHIN_5Y` becomes stable enough for historical review-only approval.
- `RB_T6_WITHIN_5Y` remains too sensitive to era/holdout behavior and fails the
  large-bucket calibration guard.

The result should not be smoothed into a blanket approval. Field-level gates are
still required.
