# Small Formula Pilot Advancement Rules

No candidate can become production-approved in the small review-only pilot.

## Review-Only Promise

A candidate may be called `promising review-only` only if it:

- compares against PYF / prior-year points.
- reports overall and position-level metrics.
- beats PYF or clearly contextualizes where it does not beat PYF.
- does not materially worsen sparse-history or low-games slices.
- improves or explains PYF false positives or false negatives.
- uses only allowed review-safe inputs.
- passes leakage/as-of checks.
- documents source/use-gate status.
- leaves rankings, runtime behavior, source gates, and production files unchanged.

## What Advancement Cannot Mean

Advancement cannot mean:

- production/model-use approval.
- ranking integration.
- app behavior change.
- formula winner selection.
- Formula Gauntlet clearance.
- 100-candidate Gauntlet clearance.
- champion refinement clearance.
- exact Model v4 replay clearance.
- source promotion.

Any candidate that fails PYF may still be evidence if it explains a narrow predeclared slice without broader harm, but it cannot be called a winner.
